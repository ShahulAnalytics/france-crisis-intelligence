# Source register

These primary-source portals are starting points. Verify exact dataset, series code, units, revision policy and coverage before using any value.

- INSEE: https://www.insee.fr/
- Eurostat: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/
- European Central Bank Data Portal: https://data.ecb.europa.eu/
- Banque de France: https://www.banque-france.fr/
- DARES: https://dares.travail-emploi.gouv.fr/
- France Travail: https://www.francetravail.org/
- French Ministry of Agriculture: https://agriculture.gouv.fr/
- French Ministry of Economy: https://www.economie.gouv.fr/
- European Commission: https://commission.europa.eu/
- OECD Data: https://data.oecd.org/
- World Bank Data: https://data.worldbank.org/

Secondary news sources can help discover events, but significant claims should be traced to original documents or independently corroborated. Inclusion here does not mean every claim from a source is verified.
