# Executive summary — France Crisis Intelligence

This repository provides an evidence-first framework for monitoring selected French economic and social indicators. It is not, by itself, evidence that France is in a national crisis.

## Monitoring areas

- Consumer price inflation, including food categories.
- Employment and purchasing-power context.
- Selected euro exchange-rate observations.
- Source-linked event ledgers and transparent baseline forecasts.

## Interpretation

- Inflation rates differ from price levels.
- Food-price pressure does not establish physical shortages.
- Exchange rates reflect euro-area and global factors, not France alone.
- Statistical alerts flag unusual values, not causes.
- Loss estimates require a stated counterfactual and checks for overlap.
- Forecasts are conditional scenarios and should be compared with simple baselines.

## Current status

The root dashboard uses illustrative demo data until verified feeds are integrated. The extended project includes ingestion and reporting components; review feed status and data-quality metadata before using outputs for policy, media or financial decisions.
