# France Crisis Intelligence — Evidence Review

**Status:** Technical monitoring report, not a declaration that France is experiencing a crisis.

## Executive finding

A defensible assessment of France's economic and social risks must compare verified indicators over time and distinguish observed conditions from media claims, estimated impacts and forecasts. No single inflation figure, exchange-rate move, protest, supply report or forecast establishes a national crisis.

## Prices and food affordability

Use official HICP/CPI series and show both inflation rates and index levels. Compare food-price changes with real disposable income where compatible measures exist. Food-price inflation is not proof of empty shelves or household food insecurity.

## Employment and income

Use official labour-market and household-income statistics with definitions, revisions and seasonal adjustment documented. Interpret unemployment, vacancies and nominal wages alongside real wages, participation, hours worked and household composition.

## Currency and external prices

State exchange-rate quotation direction and unit. ECB reference rates are not retail transaction quotes. The euro reflects euro-area monetary conditions, trade, risk sentiment and global developments; attributing a move solely to France requires stronger causal evidence.

## Supply disruptions

Record events only with date, location, affected goods, duration, direct source and corroboration. Separate local temporary stockouts from national availability indicators.

## Losses and forecasts

Each loss estimate needs scope, period, currency/base year, method, counterfactual, source, uncertainty and overlap group. Start forecasts with naive and seasonal-naive baselines, use time-ordered validation, and report uncertainty. Structural breaks and policy responses can make extrapolation unreliable.

## Data quality gates

Verify source URL and retrieval time; units and geography; period and seasonal adjustment; missingness and duplicates; revisions; forecast evaluation; and whether the conclusion exceeds what the data support.

## Limitations

Some data and ledger rows may be illustrative or incomplete until ingestion succeeds. Empty datasets mean data are unavailable, not that no event occurred. News feeds are discovery tools, not fact-checking systems. Generated findings require human review before external publication.
